Dates come from a third-party calendar. If none are shown, check the company or stock exchange website.

What do these numbers mean?
  • Price change: how much the share price has changed since the previous close. A plus or green number means it went up; a minus or red number means it went down.
  • Today’s low and high: the lowest and highest prices reported for the current trading session.
  • Trading volume: the number of shares traded during the session.
  • Company results: a planned date when a company may announce its financial results. Dates are not guaranteed.
